All mod configs are accessible at the bottom right of the screen.

There are two ways you can open this panel.
1. Configure and use the "Quick Mod Config" keybind
2. Add the tool with a gear icon if DragonLens mod is installed

Features
- Config panel is at bottom right of screen keeping the center of screen visible
- The panel remembers where you left off even if you leave/re-join your world
- Config edits are saved in realtime, no need to press a "Save Config" button

Known Issues
- Mods are showing up even if they don't have any mod configs. I will need to gray out / disable the buttons but still show them.
- Color and List types are not supported yet
